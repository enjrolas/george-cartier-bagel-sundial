This is a project dedicated to using the monument to George-Etienne Cartier in Montreal as a gnomon, and interpreting the shadow of the angel's outstretched arm on the monument as a vector pointing you to the bagel shop in Montreal that you should go to.  Monsieur Cartier was known as a connoisseur of bagels, and his advice persists from beyond the grave.
The shadow will change direction over time, which indicates M. Cartier's advice changing -- perhaps the mornings are best

the main page renders a view from the point of view of the statue, /ar lets you place a george cartier bagel sundial on the ground in front of you, and /watch is for those unfortunate people who are not in montreal, but still want a good bagel -- /watch uses AR to render a george cartier sundial on your wrist, and uses the angle of the shadow at george cartier's monument back in montreal to point you towards your nearest local bagel shop, based on your geolocated position.

Future ideas for improvement:  Viateur Bagel and Fairmount are both open 24 hours a day.  At night, when no visible shadow is available, we could do our best to interpret the shadow cast by the _serpens_ constellation as M. Cartier's guidance, since a serpent is just one fatal mistake away from an ourobouros, the official mascot of the bagel.
