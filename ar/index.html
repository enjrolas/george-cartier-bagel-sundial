Bagel Sundial AR

Stand at the Monument George-Étienne Cartier and plant a milled-steel bagel-sundial dial on the ground. The shadow sweeps the dial and points you to a Montréal bagel shop.

Requires being at the statue · Android Chrome (ARCore) · camera + location.
